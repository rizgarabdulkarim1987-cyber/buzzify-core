## Rulings

- R6-1 States: `REQUESTED` (the receiving branch asks, or the sender prepares a push) -> `SENT` (stock has left the sender) -> `RECEIVED` (received = sent on every line) or `PARTIAL` (closed with differences, every difference has a reason) ; `CANCELLED` only from REQUESTED (a SENT transfer cannot be cancelled: it must be received, with the difference recorded). The spec's separate IN_TRANSIT is the SENT state; no extra state. Cost if wrong: one enum value later.
- R6-2 In transit is a DERIVED quantity, not a stock bucket. Sending books a ledger `TRANSFER_OUT` that moves the quantity out of the sender's AVAILABLE bucket; receiving books `TRANSFER_IN` into the destination's AVAILABLE (or QUARANTINE for a line marked cold-chain-broken/damaged: see R6-3). Between the two the goods exist only on the open `PhTransferLine` (`sentQty - receivedQty - damagedQty - lostQty` over the sent lines of SENT transfers), so they are invisible to the sale screen and FEFO by construction (not sellable) and visible on the transfer screens and the chain dashboard as "in transit". No new balance column (applyMovement stays the only balance writer). Conservation invariant, tested with independent SQL: for every batch, sum over branches of all four buckets + in transit + lost = total ever received minus sold/damaged-by-ledger. Cost if wrong: adding an `inTransit` bucket later is an additive migration.
- R6-3 Differences on receive: each line has `receivedQty` (0..sentQty) plus `damagedQty` and `lostQty`; `receivedQty + damagedQty + lostQty` must equal `sentQty` when the transfer is received (every unit is accounted for; a transfer is received once). The shortfall is never silently dropped: `damagedQty` books into the destination's DAMAGED bucket (the goods exist) and needs no reason (it is the reason); `lostQty` is recorded on the line with a `lostReason` in {LOST, OTHER} (required when `lostQty > 0`, forbidden otherwise; there is no single `diffReason`) and counted in the conservation invariant and in the "lost in transit" report; closing a transfer with any LOST/OTHER shortfall needs `transfer.manage` AND `report.view` (a manager sees losses). A line received MORE than sent is refused. Lines: a REQUEST line (what was asked of a product: no batch, `requestedQty >= 1`) is a record and never changes; sending creates one SENT line per batch under it (`requestLineId`, `requestedQty` 0, `sentQty >= 1`, one line per batch per transfer, the batch of the line's own product), and the sum of the sent lines of a request line never exceeds its `requestedQty`. Send and receive are idempotent by their own key and payload hash stored on the transfer (`sendKey`/`sendHash`, `receiveKey`/`receiveHash`). Received lines also take an optional `quarantine` flag (goods arrived warm/open) booking into QUARANTINE instead of AVAILABLE. Ruling R6-3a: received units of a batch that is recalled, blocked or expired by the time they arrive go to QUARANTINE whatever the flag says, and the line then records `quarantine = true` (so the line shows where the units went); the receiver is not asked. Ruling R6-3b: transfer numbers are `T-<branch part>-<YYYYMMDD>-<sequence>` (branch part = the last four characters of the sending branch id, upper case; the sequence is per sending branch and UTC day); a sent transfer stores whether it was sent out of expiry order (`sendOverride`) and every replay returns the stored status and flag. Ruling R6-3c: at most 500 sent lines (batches) per transfer, enforced at send; receive accepts the same cap, so a sent transfer can always be received. The request itself still allows 100 lines.
- R6-4 Which stock can move: only AVAILABLE stock of ACTIVE, not-expired batches (`assertSellableHome`-like rule from adjustments/sales); never QUARANTINE/DAMAGED/RESERVED or recalled/blocked batches. Allocation of the requested product quantity to batches is FEFO by default with an explicit per-batch override for a pharmacist (`batch.override` required when picking out of FEFO order). The sender may send less than requested (partial), never more.
- R6-5 Branches: any two different branches of the SAME business; the actor needs `transfer.manage` and access to the branch they act for (send: sender branch; receive: destination branch; request: either). A branch manager sees transfers touching their branches only; OWNER sees all. Never a branch outside the business. Number per sending branch (`T-<branch>-<seq>` pattern like receipts: copy the receipts number lock). Idempotent by clientKey + payloadHash for every create/send/receive (phase 4 rule).
- R6-6 Concurrency and locks: lock order extends phase 4's: client key advisory (new namespaces, check unused) -> transfer row -> transfer lines (by id) -> stock rows (batchId COLLATE "C", then branch) -> number lock. Send of the last units racing a sale: exactly one wins, never negative (CHECKs). 40P01/40001/P2034 -> retryable CONFLICT.
- R6-7 Reports (all read-only SQL aggregates with bigint sums; period max 1 year; Baghdad calendar days, same rules as the supplier statement; keyset paging for row lists; money per currency, NEVER summed across currencies; cost/margin/profit columns only with `cost.view`/`profit.view` server-side): (a) sales summary by day/branch/user/payment method; (b) returns; (c) discounts (who, how much, over-limit); (d) cost and margin (net sales - cost of sold batches from `unitCostSnapshot`, margin % = per spec 9, label "estimated"); (e) stock and valuation by branch/category at batch cost (specific identification; items without cost shown as "no cost", never 0, valuation flagged incomplete); (f) expiry (reuse the phase 2 expiry queries: buckets 30/60/90 and expired); (g) damaged/quarantine/lost; (h) payables ageing (reuse phase 4 reads); (i) shifts (reuse phase 3 shift reports); (j) transfers: in transit, lost, by route. Taxes: only if the sale model carries a tax figure (check; if not, the report is omitted and the README says so). Customers: not in scope (no customer model until phase 7).
- R6-8 Chain dashboard (`/pharmacy/chain`): visible to roles with `report.view` and more than one branch (OWNER, BRANCH_MANAGER with several branches, PHARMACIST_IN_CHARGE with several, ACCOUNTANT): per-branch cards and a consolidated total for sales, margin (profit.view), stock value, expiring soon, in transit, open transfers, low stock; a branch comparison table. The consolidated figure is computed by the SAME query as the per-branch ones summed in SQL (`GROUPING SETS`/rollup or a second aggregate) and the acceptance test proves total = sum of branches with an independent query. A person only sees branches they have; the "total" is the total of the branches they see and the screen says so.
- R6-9 Exports: every report has CSV (UTF-8 BOM) and XLSX through the existing `src/server/sheets/write` helpers and `escapeCell` (formula injection), the same permission as the report, `allowReadOnly: true` (spec 4.7: exporting stays open after expiry), a row cap (50,000, refused rather than cut), and the file contains exactly what the person may see (cost columns omitted without `cost.view`).
- R6-10 Performance budgets: a report over 12 months of 100,000 sales lines completes < 2 s on the test DB with the right indexes (additive migration if an index is missing; measure with EXPLAIN and say which index); the chain dashboard < 1.5 s for 5 branches. Cost if wrong: indexes/materialised daily rollups later.
- R6-11 UI: no motion; en/ar/ckb parity (ar/ckb machine-drafted); RTL logical CSS; 375/768/1280 no horizontal scroll; axe clean in EN, AR AND ckb on every new screen and state; targets >= 44px; text AND icon for states; drafts kept for transfer entry with UnsavedBadge and leave guard; per-attempt keys; WriteBlocked read-only mode (transfers blocked, reports and exports open); charts are optional: tables first; any chart has a text equivalent and no animation.
- R6-12 Tests share ONE database: one DB test file at a time, always `BUZZIFY_DB_PORT=54503`.
- R6-12a Stock valuation counts available + RESERVED + quarantine at batch cost; damaged stock is shown apart and is not in the value (accepted by the controller, task 4 review). Reserved stock still belongs to the business (it is held for a sale or transfer, not written off). Cost if wrong: one SQL filter (`valued` in `reports-stock.ts`) and the hand figures of the valuation test.
